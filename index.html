About

Advanced reproductive medicine, delivered with warmth.

Dr. Shivangi Sharma brings advanced expertise in reproductive medicine and assisted fertility treatments, with specialised training in IVF and evidence-based fertility care.

Specialised training · ICMR, Mumbai

Genetics-informed care, from before conception onward.

Beyond fertility treatment, Dr. Shivangi has completed specialised training in medical genetics from ICMR, Mumbai — covering the full reproductive arc. Risk is understood early, screening is purposeful, and families are guided with clarity at every stage.

Stage 01
Preconception
Genetic risk assessment and counselling before pregnancy, so couples plan with the fullest picture.
Stage 02
Antenatal
Genetics-informed screening and monitoring through pregnancy to safeguard mother and baby.
Stage 03
Postnatal
Continued guidance after birth, supporting healthy outcomes for the newborn.
Clinical Expertise

The full pathway of assisted reproduction.

From the first stimulation cycle to embryo transfer and diagnostic procedures — handled with evidence-based protocols.

Ovarian Stimulation

Controlled ovarian stimulation protocols tailored to each patient's response.

Follicular Monitoring

Precise tracking of follicle development to time interventions accurately.

Ovum Pick-Up (OPU)

Safe, ultrasound-guided egg retrieval as a core step of the IVF cycle.

IVF / ICSI & FET

In-vitro fertilisation, intracytoplasmic sperm injection, and frozen embryo transfer.

Endometrial Preparation

Optimising the uterine lining to give each transfer the best chance of implantation.

Embryo Transfer

Precise placement of the embryo into the uterine cavity, guided by ultrasound for optimal implantation outcome.
